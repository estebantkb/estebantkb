### 🕳️ About Me
I'm a Software Engineering student based in Ecuador. I like understanding how things actually work under the hood—from designing solid backend architectures and relational databases to squeezing every drop of performance out of my hardware. 

When I'm not writing code or optimizing my setup, you'll probably find me grinding games, listening to good rock music, or just living life offline.

- ⚙️ **Core Focus:** Backend Development (Java / Spring Boot) & APIs.
- 🖥️ **Systems & Hardware:** Linux power user (Fedora) and PC enthusiast.
- 🧠 **Currently exploring:** Distributed architectures and containerization.
- 🎮 **Downtime:** Video games, rock anthems, and good anime.
